# Research archive (record only, do not run)

These scripts are kept as a record of the paused university research work.
They are not used by the app, CI or the current import tools under
`tool/timetable/`.

Do not run them. Several overwrite tracked assets under `assets/timetable` or
`assets/academic` without a backup, replace the local test app
(`~/Applications/Daily Test.app`, iOS simulator, Android device), stop or
restart running processes, or rewrite other scripts and documents. Many read
`work/` or `/tmp/` paths that do not exist in a fresh checkout (#859).
